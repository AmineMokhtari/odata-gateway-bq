**Target:** Chief Data Officers, Head of Data/Analytics, IT Directors

**The Pitch (30 Seconds):**

"Right now, your business users—Finance, Supply Chain, Marketing—are locked out of your biggest data asset: BigQuery. Because they don't know SQL, they rely on Data Engineers for custom exports, creating a massive bottleneck we call the 'SQL Tax.' 

Worse, Microsoft pushes you to constantly copy your "last mile" data into Azure Data Fabric simply to connect it to spreadsheets.

**The OData Gateway for BigQuery** solves this instantly. It’s a zero-trust data bridge that transforms BigQuery into an enterprise data marketplace for business users. It lets your business teams query petabyte-scale data *directly* from the tools they’ve already mastered—**Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and Copilot**—without moving any data, without writing a single line of SQL, and with built-in cost controls to ensure they never run up your cloud bill. 

We bring the power of Google Cloud directly to their desktop, securely and seamlessly."
